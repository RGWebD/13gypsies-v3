About this restaurant
Charming
Neighborhood gem
Good for special occasions
13 Gypsies is a cozy, intimate Spanish tapas restaurant. You can also find the influence of other cuisines throughout the menu. Primarily utilizing Spanish techniques to prepare both creative and classic renditions of Spanish dishes, Chef Kevan and his crew operate a true craft kitchen. We prepare and cook most everything from scratch including breads, cured meats, cheeses, sausages, spice blends, pickled vegetables, and desserts. This practice of making nearly everything in house promotes waste-free cooking and higher quality food. Our wines are mainly from Spain, and our beer is Florida local. We are located in a two-story Spanish-style building built in 1923 by Henrietta Dozier, Jacksonville's only female architect in the early part of the 20th century. You will find eclectic, maximalist religious iconography adorning the walls and original floor tile. We have few tables, so reservations are highly recommended. Last seating is an hour and a half before closing time.

FAQs
What are the portion sizes like at this restaurant?

13 Gypsies serves tapas-style small plates meant for sharing, and guests report that ordering several dishes usually leaves the table comfortably full.

Is this a good pick for date night?

When is the best time to go to 13 Gypsies?

For a lovely outdoor vibe, plan dinner at 13 Gypsies around sunset, guests specifically mention the sidewalk seating being “quite nice with a sunset bonus.”

What is the dress code for 13 Gypsies?

Casual dress is the guide, comfortable, everyday clothes are entirely appropriate.
