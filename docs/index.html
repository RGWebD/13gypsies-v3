Out with the new,
in with the old.

Seven tables, twenty seats and a kitchen that still kneads its bread by hand every morning. They own no mixers.

Chef Howard Kirk opened 13 Gypsies in 2008 to cook the simple Spanish food he grew up on. Chef Kevan Kimball runs the kitchen today, and almost everything is still made in house: sausage, cheese, cured meats, pickles, spice blends and fresh pasta. When a dish runs out, it’s gone for the night. First come, first eat.

“If you start with good ingredients, preparation is minimal and the end result is good, honest food.”

The room

A 1923 corner on Stockton

A two-story Spanish-style building designed by Henrietta Dozier, Jacksonville’s only female architect in the early twentieth century.

Inside: the original floor tile, gilt-framed saints and icons, a Last Supper in silver relief, and a handful of sidewalk tables out front. Small, close and a little lively, the way a neighborhood bistro should be.
